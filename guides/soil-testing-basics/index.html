Soil Testing Basics

Soil testing basics: when to test, how to take a sample, and what pH, organic matter and nutrients actually tell you.

When and how

Test before you fertilize heavily, when plants look consistently poor, or every 2–3 years for a vegetable bed. Take 10–15 small samples from the top 6 inches across the bed, mix them, and send the composite sample — one handful from one corner tells you almost nothing.

What the numbers mean

pH tells you nutrient availability, not fertility itself. Organic matter tells you about soil structure and water holding. Nutrient numbers only matter against your crop and your soil type — a number that is low for one crop can be fine for another. Follow the lab's recommendation, not a generic online chart, and retest after amending.
