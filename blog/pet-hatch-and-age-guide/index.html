Pet Hatch and Age Guide — Weight, Hatch Speed, and XP

Last updated: 4 November 2026. Pets in Grow a Garden are not just about the number on the screen. Displayed weight, effective weight, hatch time, and XP all work differently, and the calculator on this site models each one separately. This guide explains them in plain language, using the same model as the Pets calculator.

Effective Weight and the 100 kg Rule

Past 100 kg, the tool uses a damped model: only one-quarter of the extra mass over 100 kg counts toward effective weight for scaling. A pet at 120 kg therefore does not gain effective weight at the same rate as a pet at 80 kg. If you ignore damping, you will overvalue very heavy pets. Under 100 kg, the model is simpler — displayed weight and effective weight track much more closely.

Hatch Speed

Enter the base hatch time in seconds, then add any hatch speed bonus as a percentage. The tool estimates the adjusted hatch time for you. A bonus that looks small on paper can save a lot of real time over many hatches, so compare pets with and without the bonus before you choose which egg to open first.

XP Milestones

XP is cumulative: reaching a higher level needs all the XP for the levels before it, not just the last one. The Pets calculator shows cumulative XP to a target level, so you can plan how much grinding a pet actually needs. Hatch and XP are estimates — tweak the numbers to match what you see in your run, and the results will follow.

A Practical Pet Workflow

  1. Enter displayed weight and check effective weight, especially if the pet is over 100 kg.
  2. Add hatch time and bonus, and note the adjusted time.
  3. Check XP to your target level.
  4. Repeat for the other pet you are comparing.
  5. Choose based on the whole picture — weight, hatch, and XP together — not on weight alone.
