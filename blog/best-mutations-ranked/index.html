How to Read This Ranking

  • Multiplier is not the whole story: a ×500 mutation on a low base-value plant can still be worth less than a ×100 mutation on a high base-value plant. Always run the full calculation with weight and tier.
  • Stacking is additive above ×1: Shocked (×100) + Frozen (×10) = ×109, not ×1,000. That is why a stack of several mid-tier mutations can beat a single high mutation in some cases.
  • Fusions matter: some mutations only appear by combining others, such as Wet + Chilled = Frozen, and Astral = Cosmic + Galactic in the data. Check the full mutations table for fusion notes.
  • Admin means rare: 26 of the 158 entries are marked Admin in the data. They come from admin events, not normal weather, so do not plan a farm around them.

What to Chase First

If you are starting out, chase reliable weather mutations like Shocked, Celestial, and Frozen before admin-only ones. They are obtainable, they stack well, and they teach you the stacking maths without a rare drop. Once you understand stacking, use the calculator to test a stack before you commit to a trade, and use Trade Values (WFL) to check if the trade is fair.
