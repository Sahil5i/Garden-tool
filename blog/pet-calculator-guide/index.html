How the pet calculator works

The pet calculator has three main inputs: weight, hatch time, and XP. It estimates effective pet weight, hatch speed with a bonus, and cumulative XP to reach a level. It is designed for comparison — which pet to hatch, which to trade, and which to invest XP in — rather than for a single exact number.

Effective weight past 100 kg

This is the part most players miss. Past 100 kg, the tool uses a damped model: only one-quarter of the extra mass counts toward effective weight for scaling. A pet at 120 kg does not gain effective weight at the same rate as a pet at 80 kg. If you ignore damping, you will overvalue very heavy pets and undervalue the XP or hatch advantage of a lighter pet.

Hatch speed and XP

Enter the base hatch time in seconds, then add any hatch speed bonus as a percentage. The tool estimates the adjusted hatch time for you. For XP, enter the level you are aiming for and check the cumulative XP milestone. Hatch and XP are flexible estimates — tweak the numbers to match what you see in your run, and the results will follow.

A practical pet workflow

  1. Enter displayed weight and check effective weight.
  2. Add hatch time and bonus, and note the adjusted time.
  3. Check XP to your target level.
  4. Repeat for the other pet you are comparing.
  5. Choose based on the whole picture, not on weight alone.

For plant values, return to the calculator, and for pet-specific questions, see Pets.
