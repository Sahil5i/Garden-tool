Privacy Policy

This site runs as a static web application in your browser. There is no login and no account system on this site as shipped. Calculations you enter stay on your device unless you choose to share them, for example by screenshot. We do not operate a database on this site for your crop or pet inputs.

Cookies and storage

The site uses essential local storage to save your calculator preferences. By continuing, you agree to the privacy policy shown in the tool. If you clear your browser storage, your saved preferences and line items may be lost, because they are stored locally, not on a server.
