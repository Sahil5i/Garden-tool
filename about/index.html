About

Garden Calculator Pro is a free site of real-garden calculators and guides for home gardeners. Every calculator shows its formula and a worked example, so you can check the result by hand. The site is maintained as a general-information resource — it is not a substitute for local extension advice, a soil test, or a product label.

Contact: see the Contact page.

How this site is made

Garden Calculator Pro is a small, independent site of free calculators and guides for home gardeners. Every calculator shows its formula and a worked example, so you can check the result yourself. Guides are written in plain language from general gardening practice, and pages are updated when a tool changes or a reader reports a verified error. The full list of formulas is on the Methodology page.

We do not sell soil, seed or tools, and we do not take payment for calculator results. If advertising is added in future, it will be disclosed on the Privacy page before it appears. Corrections and suggestions are welcome via the Contact page.
