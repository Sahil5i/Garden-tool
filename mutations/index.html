Fruit Tier / Variant Multipliers (from the calculator)

Fruit tier is applied separately from mutations: Rainbow and Diamond ×50, Gold ×20, Silver ×5, and all other tiers ×1. A Gold Shocked plant is not Gold × Shocked in the same way as mutations stack — tier multiplies the whole result, so check it in the calculator before trading.

How Mutations Stack

Mutations stack additively above ×1 in the documented formula, then multiply the price — for example Shocked (×100) and Frozen (×10) together give ×109, not ×1,000. Some entries are fusions, such as Wet + Chilled = Frozen, and some are marked Admin, meaning they come from admin events rather than normal weather. If a modifier in the calculator is marked reference only, treat it as awareness, not as a guaranteed formula multiplier.
