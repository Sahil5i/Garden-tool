Harvest Yield Estimator

Formula

Estimated harvest = plants × yield per plant. Example: 20 plants × 2 kg each = about 40 kg. Use a yield you have actually seen for that variety, not a catalogue maximum, or your estimate will be optimistic.

Succession planting (see Succession Planting guide) spreads this harvest over weeks instead of one glut.
