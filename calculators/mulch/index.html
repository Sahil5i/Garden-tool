Mulch Calculator

Calculate mulch volume by area and depth in cubic feet, cubic yards and bags. 1 cubic yard covers 108 sq ft at 3 inches.

Mulch Calculator

Formula

Same volume formula as soil: length × width × depth ÷ 12 = cubic feet. At 3 inches, 1 cubic yard (27 cubic feet) covers 108 square feet. Example: 10 ft × 10 ft at 3 in = 25 cubic feet, just under 1 cubic yard.

Common mistakes

  • Mulch volcanoes: do not pile mulch against tree trunks or plant stems — keep it a few inches clear.
  • Too deep is also a problem: more than 4 inches can suffocate roots and stay too wet.
