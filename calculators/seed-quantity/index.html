Calculate seeds to sow from bed area and spacing, with a germination buffer. Sow extra, then thin to final spacing.

Seed Quantity Calculator

Formula

Plants = area ÷ (spacing in feet)². Seeds = plants ÷ (germination rate ÷ 100), rounded up. Example: 32 sq ft at 12 in spacing needs 32 plants; at 85% germination, sow about 38 seeds.

Germination falls with old seed and cold soil — if your seed is old, sow more, or test a few seeds on damp paper first.
