How to use this site

Pick a calculator, enter your measurements, and read the guide below the tool before you buy or spread anything. Every calculator shows its formula and a worked example, so you can check the maths by hand. Guides cover the mistakes that calculators cannot catch: mulch piled against trunks, soil that settles after watering, seed depth, and fertilizer applied to dry soil.
