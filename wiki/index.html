A calculator-backed Grow a Garden reference for crop values, plant pages, mutation pages, pets, trade values and updates. Data is generated from the calculator's verified tables (354 plants, 178 formula mutations, 158 listed mutations) and updated 4 November 2026.

  • Plants and Crops — ranked value list, base values, base weights and categories, with 354 individual plant pages below.
  • Crop Mutations — full mutation table with multipliers, plus 158 individual mutation pages below.
  • Pets — pet weight, hatch time and XP calculator, with XP time at your own XP per second.
  • Trade Values (WFL) — numeric trade calculator with Win, Fair or Lose verdict and exact difference.
  • Guides — how to calculate values, stack mutations safely and trade without overpaying.
  • Updates — site changelog and update timeline, matched to the calculator categories.

Weather, in brief: mutations in this game come from weather, pets, sprays and events — for example snow weather for Chilled, thunderstorms for Shocked, and night events for Moonlit. This site does not run a live weather tracker, because weather data goes stale in hours; the mutation pages above list how each mutation is obtained from the calculator data.
