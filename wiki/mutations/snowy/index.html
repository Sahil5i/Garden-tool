Snowy — ×2 mutation

Multiplier: ×2 · Admin mutation: No · How obtained: Snowfall weather

Snowy is a Grow a Garden crop mutation with a ×2 multiplier in the calculator data. When it is the only mutation selected, the mutation bracket is ×2. With other mutations, stacking is additive above ×1: bracket = sum of multipliers − number of mutations + 1. For example, a 1 kg Carrot (base 275 at that weight) with only Snowy is about $550 before tier, amount and friend boost.
